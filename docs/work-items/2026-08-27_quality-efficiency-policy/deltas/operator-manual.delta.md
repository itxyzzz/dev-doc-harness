## Delta

Distribution adopters select `quality-first` when stronger justified allocation
and optional independently bounded coverage are the relevant priorities. Select
`efficiency-first` when the relevant priority is the least total expected
delivery cost, including coordination and likely rework.

Both profiles inherit the generic review and authorization floor. The profile
choice may bias optional fan-out: quality-first can justify additional bounded
coverage, while efficiency-first uses the least fan-out that meets the shared
floor. Consult the canonical model and orchestration policy for the governing
rules; this delta does not restate them.
